AI for Farmers: Plant Disease Detection & Advisory System

Open-source YOLOv8 + LangChain RAG app that helps smallholder farmers detect plant diseases and get safe, farmer-friendly treatment advice.

Live Demo Poster (PNG) Manuscript Under Review · Springer Nature

Problem & Impact

  • Up to 40% of global crop yield is lost to pests and diseases.
  • Smallholder farmers often lack fast, affordable plant disease diagnostics.
  • Overuse of pesticides harms human health, ecosystems, and soil.

This project uses AI to bring fast, offline-friendly diagnosis and evidence-based advice directly to farmers in low-connectivity regions.

What I Built

  • Trained a YOLOv8m object detector on 13,519 images (20 disease classes) from the CCMT dataset.
  • Designed a retrieval-augmented advisory module with LangChain that turns extension manuals and papers into farmer-friendly guidance.
  • Deployed a Streamlit web app on Hugging Face Spaces so users can upload leaf images and instantly get disease + treatment suggestions.

System Flow

  1. 1. Upload – Farmer uploads a plant leaf photo via Streamlit UI.
  2. 2. Detect – YOLOv8 locates diseased regions and classifies the crop–disease type in real time.
  3. 3. Retrieve – Identified disease is used to query a local vector database built from extension manuals and scientific PDFs.
  4. 4. Generate Advice – LangChain assembles concise prevention and treatment tips, prioritizing non-chemical options.
  5. 5. Deliver – Clean, bullet-point guidance is displayed in the app, designed for low-bandwidth, low-literacy environments.

Architecture

Offline LLM summarization builds a structured knowledge base; at runtime, YOLOv8 detects disease and a retrieval module generates farmer-friendly guidance.

Sample Output

Example: A tomato leaf image is identified as Septoria Leaf Spot. The system retrieves disease-specific recommendations and produces concise treatment and prevention guidance: prioritizing safe, low-cost, non-chemical options for farmers.
